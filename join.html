Who Is Eligible

VFW membership is open to U.S. citizens and nationals who have served honorably in the U.S. Armed Forces and who served overseas in a war, campaign, or expedition, or who have received a qualifying campaign medal or hostile-fire/imminent-danger pay.

Not sure whether you qualify? Many veterans are surprised to learn they do. Bring your DD-214 to a meeting, or check eligibility on the VFW National site.

New Members

Apply online through VFW National and choose Post 12248 as your Post, or fill out a paper application at any meeting.
