Veteran Resources

Help for veterans and families in Calvert County

In crisis? Help is available now.

  • Veterans Crisis Line Dial 988, then press 1. Text 838255, or chat at VeteransCrisisLine.net. Free, confidential, 24/7.
  • Calvert Crisis Response 1-877-467-5628, 24/7. Calvert County's local crisis line, with a mobile crisis team that can come to you.
  • Emergency Call 911.

Free help is available for veterans, service members, and their families in Calvert County. Below are the main local, state, and VA contacts for benefits, health care, counseling, housing, and more.

You never have to pay anyone to file a VA claim. Accredited service officers from the VFW, the State of Maryland, and other veterans organizations help for free.

Benefits and VA Claims

  • VFW National Veterans Service Free help from VFW-accredited service officers with disability compensation, pension, education, and appeals. vfw.org/NVS
  • Maryland Dept. of Veterans and Military Families: Service Program Free, VA-accredited benefits specialists, with Southern Maryland offices in Charlotte Hall and Waldorf. 800-446-4926, ext. 6450 · veterans.maryland.gov
  • DAV Chapter 26, Lexington Park Free VA claims help, Wednesdays 10 AM–1 PM, first come, first served. 21795 N. Shangri-La Dr. 240-925-5439
  • VA Benefits Hotline 800-827-1000 · VA.gov

Health Care

  • Charlotte Hall VA Clinic Primary care, mental health, pharmacy, lab, audiology, and women veteran care. 29640 Three Notch Rd, Charlotte Hall. Mon–Fri, 7:30 AM–4 PM. 301-884-7102
  • Lexington Park VA Clinic 45870 East Run Dr, Suite 300, Lexington Park. Mon–Fri, 7:30 AM–4 PM. Clinic details
  • Washington DC VA Medical Center 50 Irving St NW, Washington, DC. 202-745-8000
  • Apply for VA Health Care Includes toxic exposure (PACT Act) screening. 877-222-8387 · Apply online

Counseling and Mental Health

  • Annapolis Vet Center Free, confidential counseling for veterans, service members, and families, including PTSD, grief, and military sexual trauma. 100 Annapolis St, Annapolis. 877-927-8387 (24/7)
  • Maryland's Commitment to Veterans Help connecting to behavioral health care in Maryland. 877-770-4801 (24/7)
  • Calvert County Health Department 410-535-5400 · calverthealth.org

Housing and Financial Help

  • Maryland Veterans Trust Fund Short-term help with rent, mortgage, and utilities. Applications accepted the 1st–15th of each month. Learn more and apply
  • Calvert County Dept. of Social Services Food, financial, and emergency assistance, and the entry point for Project ECHO shelter. 200 Duke St, Prince Frederick. 443-550-6900
  • National Call Center for Homeless Veterans 877-424-3838 (24/7)
  • Property Tax Exemption Maryland exempts the homes of 100% disabled veterans and eligible surviving spouses. Details

Calvert County Services

  • Calvert County Veterans Services 410-535-1600, ext. 8803 · veterans@calvertcountymd.gov · County veterans page
  • Commission for Veterans and Military Families Advises the County Commissioners on veterans issues. Meets the 1st Wednesday of each month at 5 PM, 30 Duke St, Prince Frederick. Commission page
  • Calvert County Office on Aging Senior centers, Meals on Wheels, and caregiver support. 410-535-4606
  • Charlotte Hall Veterans Home Maryland's state veterans home: assisted living and skilled nursing. 301-884-8171 · charhall.org

Education, Jobs, and Burial

  • GI Bill 888-442-4551 · VA education benefits
  • Veteran Employment Help Maryland's American Job Centers have dedicated veteran staff. labor.maryland.gov
  • Cheltenham Veterans Cemetery The nearest Maryland state veterans cemetery. 11301 Crain Hwy, Cheltenham. 301-372-6398

More Resources

Calvert County publishes a full Veterans Resource Guide covering legal aid, caregivers, dental care, transportation, recreation, and more.

Fellow veterans are a resource too. Calvert County's veterans organizations include VFW Post 12248 and the American Legion posts in Chesapeake Beach (Post 206), Huntingtown (Post 85), Prince Frederick (Post 220), and Lusby (Post 274). Learn how to join Post 12248.
