About Post 12248

The George W. Owings III Post serves veterans of Calvert County and the surrounding area who have served overseas in wars, campaigns, and expeditions.

Chartered on March 28, 2026, the Post is named for Secretary George W. Owings III, a Marine Corps Vietnam veteran, Maryland delegate, and Secretary of the Maryland Department of Veterans Affairs whose dedication to those who wore our nation's uniform was unwavering.
